Chapter I · Dadu Elementary School · 2024–2025

A Pair of Owls in the Flame Tree
Nature's Most Beautiful Parenting Story

The nest box went up at sunset. By midnight, the owls had already moved in.

On the evening of October 22, 2024, a handmade nest box was secured high up in a flame tree beside the schoolyard at Dadu Elementary School in Hsinchu's Hengshan Township. Everyone expected wild birds to take weeks — maybe months — before they'd trust something built by human hands. That same night, the first Collared Scops Owl arrived. It tilted its head, peered inside, and quietly claimed the box as home — setting off a two-year story that nobody could have predicted.

CHAPTER ONE
A Love Story, and Three Little Eggs
Oct 22, 2024
Late night
The nest box went up that evening — and an owl showed up that very night. Nature surprised everyone with a gift nobody expected.
Mar 7, 2025
Official move-in day. The male and female settled in together. The male began hunting and bringing food to the female — a textbook division of roles that would continue for months.
Mar 14, 2025
onwards
The eggs arrive. The female laid the first egg, then one more every 2–3 days — three white eggs in total, each tucked carefully under her warm feathers.
Apr 17, 2025
The first chick hatches! The mother gently ate the eggshell and began grooming her tiny newborn. The other two followed within a day or two.
A nest box is a form of ecological compensation

Collared Scops Owls are an indicator species of healthy lowland habitats — but natural tree hollows are disappearing fast as forests shrink and cities grow. A nest box gives them a second chance. Every box that WNC employees built with their own hands is one more door opened for a family looking for a safe place to raise their young.

Between hatching and fledging, the father owl worked tirelessly — hunting through the night to keep three hungry chicks fed:

🐸 frogs 🐭 mice 🦎 lizards 🦎 geckos 🦇 bats 🦗 stick insects 🦗 katydids
🏆
Record: 50 food deliveries in a single night.
Dad owl's dedication gave all three chicks everything they needed to grow strong enough to fly.
May 8, 2025

May 10, 2025
They begin practicing their wings — three little heads crowding the entrance, watching the kids play in the schoolyard far below.

As the last light fades, all three chicks spread their wings and leap into the night sky — their very first flight. Mission accomplished. 🎉
The nest box is empty now. But somewhere in the dark forests of Hsinchu, three young owls are out there — keeping watch over the night.
