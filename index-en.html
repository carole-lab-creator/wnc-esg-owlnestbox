ABOUT THIS PROJECT
A little background

For two years running, WNC's Mechanical and Industry Design Div. has quietly driven a sustainability initiative entirely on its own — no mandate, just heart. The team sponsored network cameras at 11 local schools across Hsinchu, and hand-crafted 18 nest boxes for the Collared Scops Owl (Otus lettia), a native species that's losing nesting spots as old trees disappear.

The boxes were installed by a local woodland expert — Zhu Feng Le Jing Shao Tan Wo — and every single one was made by WNC employees themselves. We chose Dadu Elementary School and Xinfeng Junior High School as the two flagship stories: one that went beautifully, and one that tested everyone's faith in life's resilience.

The Collared Scops Owl is an indicator species of healthy lowland habitats. When they move in, it means the ecosystem is doing okay. These owls chose WNC's boxes. That says something.
